**Gydr** (pronounced "guider") lets businesses and agencies create their own AI chatbots without writing code. Teach it your documents, products, FAQs and store locations, and it answers customers in English, Malay and Chinese, then hands the conversation to your team when a person is needed.

### What you can do with Gydr

- **Answer customers on every channel** — your website, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and Telegram.
- **Ground every answer in your own knowledge** — documents, product catalogues, FAQs and locations, with guardrails that check replies before they are sent.
- **Hand off to people** — live chat or tickets when the AI should step back.
- **Recommend products** — conversational search over your catalogue.
- **Resell it as your own** — agencies run chatbots for their clients under their brand.
